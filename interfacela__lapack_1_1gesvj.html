The SVD of A is written as [++] [xx] [x0] [xx] A = U * SIGMA * V^*, [++] = [xx] * [ox] * [xx] [++] [xx] where SIGMA is an N-by-N diagonal matrix, U is an M-by-N orthonormal matrix, and V is an N-by-N unitary matrix.
